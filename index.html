How Wordle Arena Works

1. 5 Words in a Row: Every player in the room solves the exact same sequence of 5 secret 5-letter words.

2. Shared Pool of 25 Tries: You have 25 total guesses across all 5 words combined. Solve early words quickly to save tries for tougher words!

3. Live Colleague Slots: Every colleague who joins opens a live mini-board showing their green/yellow/gray tile progress in real time—without revealing their letters.

Green: Right letter in the right spot.
Yellow: Letter is in the word, wrong spot.
Gray: Letter is not in the word.
